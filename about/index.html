Cabarete Beach Dominican Republic

About this guide

Updated November 10, 2026

Cabarete Beach Dominican Republic is an independent travel guide to Cabarete and the nearby North Coast of the Dominican Republic: Kite Beach, Playa Encuentro, the town beach, El Choco National Park, restaurants, nightlife and day trips. It is published by Eye To Ad Media, a small web publisher based in Denver, Colorado.

What we are, and aren't

We're a guide, not a tour operator or travel agent, and we're not affiliated with the Dominican Republic Ministry of Tourism or any government office. We don't take bookings or payments for tours, lessons or rooms. For anything you book, the business you book with is responsible for the service.

How we put pages together

  • Prices, opening hours, park fees and entry rules change often. Where a page states one, check it with the business or the official source before you travel.
  • Safety information on Is Cabarete safe? is general visitor guidance. Always check the current official travel advice (U.S. State Department, Government of Canada, UK FCDO) before you go.
  • Season and wind information (for kitesurfing and surfing) describes typical patterns, not a forecast.
