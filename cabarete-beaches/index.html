Cabarete Beach

Cabarete Beach is the town’s social shoreline. The official tourism site describes it as a place for wind sports as well as swimming and relaxing, with restaurants, bars, shops and lounges on the eastern side. It is the natural starting point for a first visit because beach time and town amenities sit together.

Kite Beach

Kite Beach is the wind-sports-focused western side of the Cabarete beach area. It is especially appealing to kiteboarders and people who want to watch the sport. Respect active launch and landing areas and use local guidance before entering the water.

Playa Encuentro

Playa Encuentro is the surf-focused choice near Cabarete. It appears prominently in the Dominican Republic’s 2026 Cabarete Surf & Wind City campaign. Surfers should match the day’s conditions to their ability and beginners should consider instruction.

Beyond Cabarete

The north coast makes it easy to turn Cabarete into a base rather than a single-beach vacation. Sosúa is known for its bay, swimming, snorkeling and diving, while Puerto Plata combines beaches with historic and cultural attractions. Conditions vary by beach, so evaluate each location independently.
