Where Cabarete fits on the north coast

Cabarete is on the Dominican Republic’s north coast in Puerto Plata province. It works well for travelers whose priorities include surfing, kitesurfing, beaches and an active beachfront town. Nearby Sosúa and Puerto Plata add diving, city history and other north-coast experiences.

Getting to the region

Gregorio Luperón International Airport serves the Puerto Plata area. The official tourism site identifies it as the region’s main air gateway. Travel times vary with traffic and your exact destination, so confirm transportation with your lodging or transfer provider before arrival.

Entry planning

Entry requirements can change. The Dominican Republic tourism authority provides current travel-planning information and directs travelers to the country’s E-TICKET process. Use official sources before departure rather than relying on an old blog post for immigration requirements.

A flexible three-day outline

Day one can focus on Cabarete Beach and town. Day two can be built around surfing at Playa Encuentro or kiteboarding with appropriate instruction. Day three can add caves, nature or a north-coast day trip. Treat that as a framework, not a schedule: ocean and weather conditions should influence the order.

Stay longer if activities are the priority

Wind and waves do not follow vacation calendars. Extra days give active travelers more chances to match conditions to their ability. They also leave room for relaxed beach time, restaurants and nearby destinations without rushing.
